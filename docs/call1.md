## Eligibility criteria under Call#1

Startups and SMEs that fulfill the following criteria can apply for Call#1:

- Are legally incorporated and established in one of the EU member states
- Are nominated SME according to the EU definition (Commission Recommendation 2003/361/EC)
- Have not received over 200,000 € in state aid support during the previous 3 fiscal years
- Have an AI and robotics solutions with a minimum Technology Readiness Level (TRL) of 4
- Were not supported or are under supported by another Portuguese testbed
